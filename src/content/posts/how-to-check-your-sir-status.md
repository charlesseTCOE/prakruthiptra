Karnataka's Special Intensive Revision (SIR) has flagged over 1.1 crore voters under the **ASDDO** category — Absent, Shifted, Duplicate, Dead, or Others. Being flagged doesn't mean you're removed from the rolls, but it does mean your record needs confirming before the final list is published. It's worth checking — it only takes a minute.

> **Quick check:** enter your EPIC (Voter ID) number at [karnataka-asddo-lookup.vercel.app](https://karnataka-asddo-lookup.vercel.app/) to see instantly if your name is on the ASDDO list. It's a community-built lookup tool, not a government site — enter only your Voter ID number, nothing else, and treat it as a fast first check rather than the final word.
>
> **If your name shows up, contact your BLO (Booth Level Officer) immediately** so it can be corrected before the deadline.

## The official way to check

1. Go to [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in)
2. Select **Special Intensive Revision (SIR) – 2026**
3. Search by your name or Voter ID
4. If you haven't already, fill the enumeration form from the same portal

You can also browse the district-wise ASDDO lists directly on the Chief Electoral Officer, Karnataka's site: [ceo.karnataka.gov.in](https://ceo.karnataka.gov.in)

## Key dates

- **17 August 2026** — last date for house-to-house verification and enumeration form submission
- **24 August 2026** — draft electoral roll published
- **24 August – 23 September 2026** — claims & objections window
- **27 October 2026** — final electoral roll published

Residents with questions can also reach out to the committee directly, and we'll help point you in the right direction.

CEO Karnataka’s press note dated **18 September 2026** records **4,47,39,070** electors on the roll that day. Enumeration closed **17 August**; draft published **24 August**. Claims close **23 September**. Last hearing **12 October**; disposal **22 October**; final roll **27 October**. Search by EPIC on voters.eci.gov.in. ASDDO lists: https://ceo.karnataka.gov.in/asddo.html.

Being marked **ASDDO** (Absent, Shifted, Dead, Duplicate / already enrolled, or Other) does **not** by itself delete you. It means the record still needs a hearing or a form. Do not wait for a notice if you already know you are missing.

> **Official check:** [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) → Special Intensive Revision (SIR) 2026 → search by EPIC / name.
>
> District lists and notices: [ceo.karnataka.gov.in](https://ceo.karnataka.gov.in)
>
> A fast community lookup also exists at [karnataka-asddo-lookup.vercel.app](https://karnataka-asddo-lookup.vercel.app/) — EPIC number only; treat it as a first check, not the final word.

## What to file

- **Form 6** — inclusion (new voter, or you were left off the draft)
- **Form 6A** — overseas elector
- **Form 7** — object to a name that should not be on the roll
- **Form 8** — correction of existing details, or after you receive a notice

Use your EPIC number when you search.

## Dates that still matter (as of 18 September 2026)

- **19–20 September** — special Form 6 registration camps
- **23 September** — last date to file claims and objections on the draft
- **12 October** — last date scheduled for hearings on notices
- **22 October** — last date to dispose of claims, objections and notice cases
- **27 October** — final electoral roll
- Qualifying date for this revision: **1 October 2026**

If a notice reaches you after 23 September, you can still appear for the hearing — notices and hearings run into October. File Form 8 if you also need a correction.

## If you live in Prakruthi Township

Check the draft this week. If your name is missing or marked shifted / unmapped, take EPIC + address proof to the BLO or the Form 6 camp on 19–20 September. Write to the committee at prakruthiptra@gmail.com if you need the local booth contact.

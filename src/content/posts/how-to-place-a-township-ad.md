---
title: "How to place an advertisement on the PTRA site"
date: "2026-09-18"
excerpt: "No public listings until the committee publishes one. Email the copy; do not expect the Jobs form to create an ad."
---

The classifieds board starts empty on purpose. Sample ads have been removed.

To request a listing:

- Email **prakruthiptra@gmail.com** with subject `Advertise`
- Headline, 40–80 words, a phone number people may call
- Say whether it is Housing, Services, Jobs, Events or Community

The committee checks the copy and then adds it to the site. Partner banners are a separate quarterly slot — ask in the same mail.

Jobs applications (note + file) are **not** public ads. Those go only into the private Drive folder.

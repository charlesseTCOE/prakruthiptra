In an emergency, finding a number should be the easy part. Save the contacts below on your phone and keep a written copy somewhere everyone at home can find it.

## Start with the right number

- **112:** unified emergency assistance for police, fire, medical and other urgent incidents.
- **108:** emergency ambulance assistance.
- **101:** fire service, listed in the Bengaluru Urban district directory.
- **1091:** women’s helpline, listed by the district.
- **1098:** child helpline, listed by the district.

## Make a small household contact card

Write down your address, nearest landmark, a trusted contact and any access instructions responders may need. Let family members know where the card is kept. Share personal information only with those who need it.

When calling, describe what happened and where. Answer the operator’s questions and follow their instructions. Do not wait for a reply from the association or a neighbourhood social group before calling emergency services.

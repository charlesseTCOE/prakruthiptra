A useful complaint gives the service team a clear location and a short description. Before contacting BWSSB, note what you can observe safely.

## Keep these details ready

- RR number from your water bill, if available.
- Street, block and a recognisable landmark.
- The issue: no supply, low pressure, visible leakage or sewage overflow.
- When you first noticed it and whether nearby homes report the same issue.
- A photo taken from a safe place, if useful.

BWSSB lists **1916** as its call-centre number. Its website also lists **14420** for Safai Mitra; use the main call centre if you are unsure which service is appropriate.

You can also email **callcenter@bwssb.gov.in**, as listed on BWSSB’s official helpline page. Include your RR number and location privately.

## Save the reference number

Ask for a complaint reference and keep it with the date of your call. When following up, describe what has changed and quote the same reference. Avoid posting your bill or personal contact details publicly.

For a problem affecting several homes, share a concise summary and the complaint reference with PTRA through the [contact section](/#contact). This helps the association understand the common issue without circulating everyone’s personal records.

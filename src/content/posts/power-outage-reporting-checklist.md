When the power goes off, a clear report helps distinguish an individual connection issue from a wider interruption. Check with nearby residents whether they are affected, without entering electrical areas or touching equipment.

## Prepare your report

Have your electricity account ID, address, landmark and the approximate start time ready. Describe whether the entire home or only part of it is affected, and mention any known neighbourhood interruption.

BESCOM’s official Contact Us listing gives **1912** as its 24-hour helpline and **9449844640** for WhatsApp. Keep your electricity account ID ready. Use the official contact page to check for changes.

## Keep the follow-up simple

Ask for a complaint reference. Note any update the operator provides, but avoid passing on a restoration time as confirmed unless BESCOM has actually given one. Share the source and time when forwarding an outage announcement to neighbours.

For immediate danger, call **112**. Stay away from fallen wires and damaged electrical equipment; leave inspection and repair to qualified personnel.

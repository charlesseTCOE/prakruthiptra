Use **Write to us** for anything that is not an emergency (those still go to 1533 / 1916 / 1912 / 101 / 108).

1. Open [Write to us](/apply).
2. Pick a topic and describe the block, landmark and when you noticed it.
3. Submit. The note is saved in the committee’s **Google Drive folder**. It is not emailed to prakruthiptra@gmail.com automatically.

Office-bearers read the folder (or `/admin` after signing in). If the matter is urgent after dark, also tell main-gate security.

Do not attach a file on this form. Files are accepted only on the **Jobs** path.

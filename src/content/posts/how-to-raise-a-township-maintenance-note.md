---
title: "How to send a maintenance note to the committee"
date: "2026-09-18"
excerpt: "Streetlight, leak, waste pile or gate issue — prepare an email to PTRA through Write to us."
---

Use **Write to us** for anything that is not an emergency (those still go to 1533 / 1916 / 1912 / 101 / 108).

1. Open [Write to us](/apply).
2. Pick a topic and describe the block, landmark and when you noticed it.
3. Select **Prepare email**, then **Open email app**. Review the draft and press **Send** to email **prakruthiptra@gmail.com**.

Office Bearers receive the message in the PTRA mailbox. Preparing a draft does not send the message or save it in the website Admin inbox. If no email app opens, expand the copy-details option and paste the message into your email service.

Attach any relevant photo or document in your email app before sending. The website does not upload files in this mode.

---
title: "Form 6 camps this weekend — 19 and 20 September"
date: "2026-09-18"
excerpt: "CEO Karnataka has scheduled special camps on 19–20 September for inclusion and corrections before the 23 September claims deadline."
---

The Chief Electoral Officer, Karnataka confirmed special weekend camps on **19 and 20 September 2026** for Form 6 (inclusion), Form 7 (objection / deletion) and Form 8 (correction).

Use a camp if:

- your name is missing from the **24 August** draft roll
- you were marked absent or shifted during house-to-house SIR
- you turn 18 by the qualifying date of **1 October 2026**
- a spelling or address line is wrong

Carry EPIC if you have one, age proof, address proof and a passport photograph. Search first on [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) by EPIC number — part and serial numbers may have changed after polling-station rationalisation.

Claims filed from 24 August to **23 September** are meant for the final roll on **27 October**, after ERO verification. Form 6 can also be filed later in the year, but that cycle is slower.

Official note dated 18 September 2026: [ceo.karnataka.gov.in](https://ceo.karnataka.gov.in). ASDDO lists: [ceo.karnataka.gov.in/asddo.html](https://ceo.karnataka.gov.in/asddo.html).

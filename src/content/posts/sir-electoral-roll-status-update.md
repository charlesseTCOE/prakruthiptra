The Election Commission of India is running a Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls across Karnataka. Every voter's record is being re-verified, so it's worth checking your own status directly.

**Check your status here:** [voters.eci.gov.in](https://voters.eci.gov.in) — select **Special Intensive Revision (SIR) – 2026**, then search by your name or Voter ID, or fill the enumeration form if you haven't already.

You can also check the Chief Electoral Officer, Karnataka's website for draft rolls and updates: [ceo.karnataka.gov.in](https://ceo.karnataka.gov.in)

## If your name is flagged

Some voters are being marked under the **ASDDO** category — Absent, Shifted, Duplicate, or Dead — during verification. Being flagged doesn't mean you're removed from the rolls; it means your record needs confirming.

- If you're still at your registered address, you can submit a correction before the draft rolls are finalised.
- Contact your local Booth Level Officer (BLO) if you're unsure what to do next.

## Key dates to keep in mind

- Claims & objections window: open until **16 September 2026**
- Final electoral roll publication: **19 October 2026**

Residents with questions can also reach out to the committee directly, and we'll help point you in the right direction.

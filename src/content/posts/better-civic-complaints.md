A short, specific report is easier to follow up than a long conversation with missing details. For a road, drain, streetlight or waste issue, try this structure.

## Five things to include

1. **Location:** street, block, nearest landmark and a map pin if the service accepts it.
2. **Problem:** what you can see, in one or two sentences.
3. **Timing:** when you noticed it and whether it is recurring.
4. **Evidence:** a clear photo, without unrelated faces or personal details.
5. **Reference:** an earlier complaint number, if one exists.

The Greater Bengaluru Authority’s Public Relations account lists **1533** for civic issues. Ask the operator which corporation handles your location; do not assume an older ward assignment still applies.

## Follow up on the same issue

Keep the complaint number and record your follow-up dates. If marked resolved but still present, use the portal’s available follow-up route or call with the reference. Share the record with PTRA if it affects the wider community.

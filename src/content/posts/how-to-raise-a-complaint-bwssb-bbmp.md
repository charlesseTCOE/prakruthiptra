Residents often ask the committee how to escalate issues directly to the authorities. Here's the official way to do it for the two bodies residents deal with most.

## BWSSB (water supply, sewage, drainage)

1. **Call the helpline** — dial **1916**, give your RR (consumer) number, and describe the issue. Ask for a complaint reference number.
2. **File online** through the BWSSB Complaint Management System: [cms.bwssb.gov.in](https://cms.bwssb.gov.in/module/complain/new_complaint)
3. **Email** — bwssbcallcenter@gmail.com or bwssbcms@gmail.com, with your RR number and address.
4. **Still unresolved?** Escalate through the Karnataka Government's Janaspandana (IPGRS) public grievance system, or contact the concerned Executive Engineer directly.

BWSSB's official site has more detail: [bwssb.karnataka.gov.in](https://bwssb.karnataka.gov.in/english)

## BBMP (roads, garbage, drains, street lights, and other civic issues)

1. **File through Sahaaya 2.0**, BBMP's unified grievance portal — it covers BBMP, BESCOM, BWSSB, BMTC, BMRCL and more from one place: [sahaaya2.bbmpgov.in](https://sahaaya2.bbmpgov.in/)
2. **Call BBMP** — 1533 (toll-free, 24x7 civic helpline for garbage, potholes, streetlights, and similar issues).
3. Note your complaint/request number so you can track status and reopen it if it's closed without being resolved.

## A few tips from experience

- Always note the reference number you're given — you'll need it to follow up or escalate.
- Take a photo of the issue (pothole, leak, garbage pile) — most portals let you attach one, and it helps get faster action.
- If a complaint is marked "resolved" but the issue isn't actually fixed, reopen it on the same portal rather than filing a fresh one — that keeps the history intact.

If enough residents on a street report the same issue, it tends to get prioritised faster — feel free to share this guide with neighbours.
